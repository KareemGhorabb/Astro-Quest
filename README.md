# Astro Quest

**An Interactive Space Exploration and Astronaut Mission Training Simulator**

> *Your Mission. Your Decisions. Humanity’s Future.*

Astro Quest is a pixel-art educational game where you command a Moon or Mars base and keep a crew of four astronauts alive. Oxygen, food and water, energy, and radiation protection: every resource matters, every decision has consequences, and one wrong move can put the whole mission at risk.

Built by **Team Tatta7 on the moon** for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, Challenge 2: *Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer*.

---

## The Idea

Most kids learn about space from textbooks. We want them to **run a Moon base.**

Instead of reading about how astronauts survive, players make the same kinds of trade-offs real missions depend on: where to spend limited energy, how to protect the crew from radiation, and what to do when equipment fails. Learning happens through play.

## How It Works

You are the mission commander. Each day of the mission you:

1. Review the state of the base and the crew.
2. Assign astronauts and resources to tasks.
3. Respond to events and emergencies.
4. Move closer to your mission objectives, or lose the crew trying.

### Resources to balance

| Resource | What it means |
|---|---|
| Oxygen | Keeps the crew breathing |
| Food and water | Keeps the crew healthy and working |
| Energy | Powers life support, the greenhouse, and the lab |
| Radiation protection | Shields the crew from solar storms and cosmic radiation |

### The crew

| Astronaut | Role | Looks after |
|---|---|---|
| Nova | Commander | Mission decisions and oxygen |
| Volt | Engineer | Energy and equipment |
| Sprout | Botanist | Food and water |
| Halo | Medic | Radiation protection and health |

### Example situations

- A **solar storm** forces the crew to shelter.
- A **dust storm** cuts solar power.
- A **life-support unit breaks** and has to be repaired.
- **Food runs short** and has to be rationed.
- **Experiments** earn discovery points but cost energy and crew time.

## Scoring

Your mission is scored on:

- **Survival**: 35%
- **Resource management**: 25%
- **Mission objectives**: 20%
- **Emergency decisions**: 10%
- **Discoveries**: 10%

> The weights above are the current design and may change as we playtest.
